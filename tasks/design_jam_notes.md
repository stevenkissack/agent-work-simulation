# Design Jam Meeting Notes - Site Manager Visibility

## :cyclone: Granola Notes

**The problem**

- The Customer runs crews across several sites and can't see, in one place, what's falling behind. Right now they open each site one at a time.
- What they need at a glance: across all sites, what's overdue and what's highest priority right now.

**What we agreed to build (POC)**

- A prominent card on the Home screen that pulls together tasks from every site that need attention:
  - Show overdue tasks and the top tasks by priority
  - Tap a task to open it in the Tasks section and see the detail
- Scope it to that. This is a POC for the Product Team to click through, not the finished feature.

**Deliverable**

- Build the POC.
